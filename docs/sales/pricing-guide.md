# Gigotter pricing guide

How to quote businesses consistently. **These are starting estimates, not market data.** Adjust after your first 2–3 gigs, once you know how long each task really takes.

## 1. The formula

```
Client price per result = Student pay + Gigotter fee (+ extras)
```

- **Student pay:** what you set as the gig's **rate** in Gigotter. Aim for students to earn **at least ₵25–35 for each hour of effort**, including travel and waiting. Fair pay keeps good students coming back and cuts fake submissions.
- **Gigotter fee:** **40–60% on top of student pay** (aim for 50%). It covers verification, writing the gig, reviewing every result, support, MoMo transfer charges and redoing rejected work.
- **Minimum project fee:** **₵500** per brief, so small jobs still cover the time spent setting them up.

## 2. Starting prices by type of work

| Work | Time per result (incl. travel) | Student pay | Client price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online / phone survey response | 5–10 min | ₵5–10 | ₵10–18 |
| In-person survey response | 10–20 min | ₵10–20 | ₵18–35 |
| Customer or business onboarding (sign-up) | 20–45 min | ₵30–60 | ₵50–100 |
| Field research / price check (per shop) | 15–30 min | ₵15–30 | ₵25–50 |
| Focus group: recruiting (per attendee) | 20–40 min | ₵20–40 | ₵35–70 |
| Focus group: attending (per participant, ~90 min) | 90–120 min | ₵50–100 | ₵90–160 |
| App test (with written feedback) | 20–40 min | ₵20–40 | ₵35–70 |
| Errand / flyer drop (per stop) | 5–15 min | ₵5–15 | ₵10–25 |
| Social post (with link as proof) | 20–60 min | ₵20–50 | ₵35–90 |

Pass through at cost (plus ~10% handling): venue, refreshments, printing, product samples, delivery fares.

## 3. Adjustments

| Situation | Change |
|---|---|
| Hard-to-find respondents (e.g. women founders, home chefs) | +20–40% |
| Spread-out locations or outside Accra/Kumasi | + transport allowance, ₵10–30 per student per day |
| Rush (under 72 hours) | +25% |
| Heavy proof (long forms, several photos, recordings) | +10–20% |
| Large volume (500+ results) | −10–15% on the fee, not on student pay |
| First pilot with a new client | Keep the price; offer a smaller quantity instead |

## 4. Payment terms

- **New clients:** 100% upfront for the pilot (you pay students before the client pays you).
- **Repeat clients:** 50% upfront, the rest when results are approved.
- **Budget cap:** quote "up to ₵X". You only invoice for approved results, never more than the cap.
- **Approval rules:** agree what counts as a valid result in the brief, and give the client **3 working days** to review. Anything not rejected by then counts as approved, so results can't be rejected without a reason.
- If you're VAT-registered, check with an accountant how to show VAT on invoices.

## 5. Worked example: Kuukup, 40 home chefs

- Task: find home chefs, take them through a 21-question survey, consent required. About 30–40 minutes each, including finding them.
- Student pay: **₵30 per chef** (hard-to-find group).
- Fee at 50%: ₵15, so **₵45 per chef**.
- 40 chefs × ₵45 = **₵1,800**, plus a transport allowance of 4 students × ₵20 = **₵80**.
- **Quote: up to ₵1,880**, paid upfront. Students' pay in Gigotter: rate ₵30, 10 per student, budget cap ₵1,200.

## 6. Setting it up in Gigotter

1. **Rate** = student pay per result (not the client price).
2. **Units per student** and **students needed** set the total; **budget cap** = rate × total results.
3. Your fee is invoiced to the client separately; Gigotter only tracks what students are owed and paid.
4. After the gig, compare **budget used** on the responses page against what the client paid, to check your margin.

## 7. Quote template

```
Hi [Name], here's the pilot plan for [Company]:

Task: [e.g. 40 home chefs surveyed in Accra and Tema, with consent]
Proof per result: [photo / GPS / contact details / answers to your questions]
Timeline: [X] days from go-ahead
Price: ₵[per result] per approved result, up to ₵[cap] in total
Includes: verified students, writing and running the gig, reviewing every result, spreadsheet of all responses
Payment: [100% upfront for the pilot]. You're only charged for approved results; anything unused is refunded or carried over.

Reply "go" and we'll have students on it within [48 hours].
```

## 8. Calibrate after each gig

After every gig, note: average time per result, % rejected, student feedback on pay, and your actual margin. If students take longer than planned, or you're seeing many rejections, raise student pay before the next quote.
